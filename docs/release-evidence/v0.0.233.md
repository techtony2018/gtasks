## Behavior and limits

- Health no longer waits for cold or expired GBrain version discovery. One
  provider runs at a time, with no queued replacement for a stuck worker.
  Validated versions retain observation/verification provenance. Probe failures
  do not expose raw output or discard a previously verified version.
- Sidebar/About distinguish pending, unavailable and last-verified versions.
  Health fetch and body consumption have a five-second deadline. Recovery uses
  a finite coalesced retry sequence, not perpetual polling; explicit About
  inspection permits another sequence. A provider resolving after the finite
  window may require that explicit check to update the displayed version.
- Readiness performs no loaders, disk reads or remote probes. The five required
  active surfaces must have fresh, issue-free evidence in this server process;
  the all-System-Tickets archive is optional. Restored snapshots are retained
  but unverified until a current-process read. Metadata expires after300s.
- Unavailable cache metadata is not falsely healthy: inspection waits at most
  50ms for the publication lock, then reports inspection_busy/HTTP503 with
  unknown fields null. Last observed reads are not a live-authentication test.
- Canonical task/goal content, raw transport errors, secrets and private paths
  are excluded from readiness. App liveness can remainHTTP200 while readiness
  isHTTP503. No replacement local canonical database or uncertain-write retry.
